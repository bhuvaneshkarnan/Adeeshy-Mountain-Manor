Crafted for Serenity in the Sirumalai Hills

The Superior Double Room is crafted for romantic escapes and quiet mountain retreats. High ceilings and full-height glass patio doors draw the emerald beauty of Sirumalai's pine canopy directly into your private haven.

Step outside onto your private cedar terrace each morning to breathe crisp 1,600m alpine air. Inside, plush custom bedding, warm timber nightstands, and a spa-grade rain shower ensure deep rejuvenation after days spent on scenic forest trails.
