Getting to the Manor

  • Scenic Hill Road: 18 picturesque hairpin bends from Dindigul base.
  • Nearest Rail: Dindigul Junction (25 km / ~50 mins).
  • Nearest Airport: Madurai International Airport (IXM) (85 km / ~2 hrs).
  • Helicopter & Chauffeur: Estate private transfers arranged upon request.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most frequently asked questions about our services, amenities and reservations.

How can I make a reservation?

You can make a reservation online through our site or by contacting us by phone or email.

Is there a possibility to cancel or change a reservation?

Yes, you can cancel or change your booking according to our terms and conditions. Contact us for more information.

What amenities do you offer at the hotel?

Our hotel offers modern SPA and fitness facilities, restaurants with fine cuisine, indoor and outdoor pools, as well as specialized services for weddings, conferences and business events.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept credit cards, debit cards and cash payments. Payments can be made online at the time of booking or upon check-in.

Is there access to wireless internet in the hotel?

Yes, all our guests have free access to high-speed wireless internet throughout the hotel.

Can I bring my pet to the hotel?

Yes, our hotel welcomes guests with pets. Small and medium-sized pets are allowed with prior request. Please contact us to confirm the availability of pet-friendly rooms. Keep in mind that there may be an additional fee for accommodating pets. Pets must be supervised at all times and should not disturb other guests.
