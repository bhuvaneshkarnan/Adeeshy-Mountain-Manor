Discover the magic of Sandanski

Sandanski is not only one of the best SPA and balneo resorts in Bulgaria, but also a city with rich cultural heritage, beautiful nature and unique climate. Here you will find the perfect balance between relaxation, health and the possibility of unforgettable journeys.

Mineral water

The healing power of mineral springs

Sandanski is known for its thermal springs, which offer proven healing properties. The mineral water here is suitable for both external and internal balneotherapy, helping with a number of health conditions.

Suitable for gastrointestinal, renal and metabolic diseases

Beneficial effect on the musculoskeletal system and skin

With a high content of beneficial minerals and trace elements

Used for wellness therapies and healing procedures

climate

Mild climate with healing properties

Sandanski is known as the “Bulgarian climatic south”, thanks to its unique location. The city offers extremely clean air, rich in oxygen and negative ions, which makes it an ideal place for climate treatment.

Helps with respiratory diseases such as asthma and bronchitis

Clean air, poor in allergens and pollutants

Mild temperatures and sunny days all year round

Proven healing effect in pulmonary and allergic conditions

SIGHTSEEING

Historical and natural treasures

In the area of Sandanski you will find sights that impress with their history, beauty and cultural value.

From the cool park with over 200 species of Mediterranean plants, to the Archaeological Museum with the remains of the Episcopal Basilica and the City Art Gallery.
